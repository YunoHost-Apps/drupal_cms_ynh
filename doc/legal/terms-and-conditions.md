## Notes before you publish — delete this section

1. **Fill every `[BRACKETED]` placeholder.** Under Article 4 of the Bulgarian E-Commerce Act (Закон за електронната търговия) you must disclose your company name, UIC, registered seat, and contact details — including a means of direct and effective contact. That is exactly what the currently-empty `/imprint` page should carry, and Section 1 above is the same content. Ask me and I'll draft the Imprint too.
2. **Rename the page and the URL.** It is currently titled "Term & Conditions" and lives at `/term-conditions`. It should be "Terms & Conditions" at `/terms-conditions`, with a redirect from the old path.
3. **Section 2 is the most important clause here** and the one most often got wrong. It stops a prospect arguing that your website's tier descriptions are contractual promises. Do not delete it, and make sure the separate services agreement it points to actually exists before you sign anyone.
4. **Section 5.2 matters commercially.** Your whole model rests on other people's open-source projects. Being explicit that you are not their official partner protects you from a trade mark complaint and from a client believing they have upstream vendor support they do not have.
5. **Section 8 deliberately splits website liability from services liability.** Your real liability exposure belongs in the services agreement, negotiated per client. Do not try to make this page do that job.
6. **Have a Bulgarian lawyer review this.** I am not a lawyer and this is a carefully researched draft, not legal advice. Sections 8 and 11 in particular need checking against Bulgarian mandatory law and against whatever your insurance requires.
